# kk-desk

A private tool shared with family and friends.

## Set it up (about 15 minutes)

1. Download **kk-desk-setup.exe** from the newest release (**Releases**, on the right of this page).
2. Open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", press **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Paste the key you were given and press **Set up**. That is all you do there: it installs itself, puts a shortcut on
   your desktop and opens in your browser.
4. Follow the steps on the page that opens. The first one installs **Antigravity** (Google's free AI helper) and signs
   you in with your Google account; after that, Antigravity helps you with the rest.

Good to know: while the program is open, your PC stays on. A laptop running on its battery can still go to sleep when
the program has nothing to do.

## Updating

When you get a newer **kk-desk-setup.exe**, run it the same way with the same key. Your own files and settings stay.
